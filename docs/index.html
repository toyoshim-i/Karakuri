Karakuri

A visual instrument that writes its own material

Procedures that run on the GPU every frame, kept in a library, loaded into decks and mixed live — and rewritable, by you or by a model, while they are on screen.

The manual → What the instrument is, the seven rules its surface obeys, and the console region by region. The repository → The source, and the development documents — roadmap, language specification, decision records.
You are at the root of this project's document tree. Everything beside the manual here is written for whoever is building Karakuri rather than for whoever is playing it, and reads better in the repository than on this site.
